2. Core Platform Modules & Features

01. Brand DNA Memory & Automated Scraper /brand-dna

Scrapes brand website URLs to ingest logos, hex color palettes, positioning statements, and verified claims. Locks guidelines into immutable single-source AI memory.

  • Automated URL identity scraper for logos, hex colors, and positioning
  • Immutable single-source AI memory preventing voice drift
  • Multi-brand client workspace isolation
02. 30-Day Strategy Roadmap Engine /strategy

Synthesizes 30 non-repeating daily social campaign directives across Instagram, LinkedIn, X, and Facebook. Integrates product photo brief analysis.

  • 30 daily non-repeating campaign directives across 4 social channels
  • Reference product photo photoshoot directive synthesis
  • 1-click conversion into image briefs and social post captions
03. Creative Studio & 2-Agent Vision Pipeline /creative-studio

Renders photorealistic 8K commercial ad visuals powered by Gemini 3.1 Flash Image AI vision models with automatic brand logo overlays.

  • Agent 1 (Prompt Crafting Agent) + Agent 2 (Image Generation Agent)
  • Photorealistic 8K commercial ad rendering
  • Automated Brand DNA logo watermarking & GCS V4 signed URL exports
04. Content Studio & Carousel Builder /content-studio

Multi-channel copywriter generating Instagram carousels, LinkedIn slide decks, viral X threads, Facebook ads, and long-form blog articles.

  • Slide-by-slide Instagram carousel & LinkedIn slide deck generator
  • Viral X thread creator & long-form article writer
  • 1-click copy repurposing & tone/emoji density adjustment
05. AI Website & Landing Page Builder /website-builder

Prompt-driven landing page generator with real-time live code execution, split visual preview, and full HTML/CSS ZIP package exports.

  • Prompt-to-full landing page HTML/CSS structure generation
  • Live code preview editor with responsive viewport switching
  • 1-click downloadable source ZIP code packages
06. SEO Intelligence & JSON-LD Briefs /seo-intelligence

Performs search intent keyword analysis, competitive SERP audits, and structured Schema.org JSON-LD markup generation.

  • Commercial & informational search intent keyword analysis
  • Structured JSON-LD schema generator (Article, Product, FAQPage)
  • Comprehensive SEO content brief outlines for top rankings
07. Omnichannel Publishing Calendar /calendar

Interactive drag-and-drop calendar grid to schedule multi-channel social publishing with smart peak engagement timing.

  • Drag-and-drop schedule grid across days and weeks
  • Live social card feed previews (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook)
  • Auto-scheduling at peak audience activity hours
08. Approvals Desk & Governance Queue /approvals-desk

Human-in-the-loop review desk for agencies and clients to inspect, verify brand claims, comment on, or approve content before publishing.

  • Human-in-the-loop quality gate and audit log trail
  • Automated AI brand claim verification checks
  • 1-click client approval & revision request workflows
09. Embedded AISA™ AI Copilot Assistant Global Assistant Drawer

Floating 24/7 AI chat copilot trained specifically on content marketing workflows, prompt optimization, and platform navigation.

  • Real-time campaign strategy advice & prompt refinement
  • Conversational shortcut triggers for all 9 platform engines
10. Analytics & Velocity Hub /analytics

Performance tracking dashboard monitoring content velocity metrics (400% gain), credit consumption, and team output ROI.

11. Team RBAC & Multi-Tenant Isolation /team-rbac

4 granular user roles (Super Admin, Agency Admin, Creator, Client) with complete data separation between client brand accounts.
